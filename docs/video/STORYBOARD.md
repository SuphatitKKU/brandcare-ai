# BrandCare Product Story — storyboard

Format: 105 seconds, 1920×1080, 60 fps. Thai text and instrumental music, no voiceover.

## Creative direction

Reference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SgmuplXU2iY — Best SaaS Product Launch Ad Video | LangEase, Zelios. Inspected the kinetic opening, floating multi-card layout, tilted phone/UI close-up and blue brand-symbol treatment. Translate those ideas into BrandCare's white/gray/blue identity with original compositions, actual current UI screenshots, soft perspective movement, overlapping transitions and deliberate reading time. Reference footage/music is not reused.

## Evidence and wording

1. **63%**, DHL E-Commerce Trends Report 2025 Business Edition. Survey: 4,050 e-commerce businesses in 19 markets. Retailers selling on 3 or more platforms, not the percentage with missed messages. https://group.dhl.com/en/media-relations/press-releases/2025/dhl-e-commerce-trends-report-2025-business-edition.html
2. **62.72%**, ETDA SME Digital Maturity Survey 2023. 1,725 Thai companies from High Value Sector and S-curve groups. Digital skills/capabilities challenge, not a representative percentage of all Thai micro online shops. https://www.etda.or.th/th/Useful-Resource/etda_sme.aspx
3. **53%**, Salesforce Small & Medium Business Trends, 6th Edition, printed page 13. 3,350 SMB leaders from 26 countries, survey August–September 2024. Inconsistent data across tools. https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/resources/smb-trends-report-6th-edition_Salesforce.pdf
4. **89%** expect a business to respond to reviews; **50%** say generic or templated responses make them unlikely to choose a business. BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026: 1,002 US adults, local-business context. These are reported attitudes, not conversion lift or Thai consumer estimates. https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/

Use the first 1.5 seconds of each counter as animated reveal, then retain the exact reported figure. Statistical claims are accompanied by source/year/population in-frame; actual URLs are supplied here and in the project evidence document.

## Product demo truthfulness

All shop, customer, sales, review and generated-response records are fictional. Website screenshots and animated extracts are mockup demonstrations. No connection, AI call or actual sending is implied. The sales/complaint relationship is shown for investigation, not as proof of causation. The final frame states the prototype's integration status.
