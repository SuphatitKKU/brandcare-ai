# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the owner of a small family-run shop who handles sales, customer replies, and marketing with a small team. The demo persona is Khun Phrae of Khanom Baan Suan.

## Product Purpose

BrandCare AI helps shop owners keep up with customer feedback across channels, understand how it relates to sales, and decide what to do next.

## Positioning

The product connects customer voice with sales performance and turns the combined signals into prioritized actions. It presents recommendations for the owner to review and approve before actions are taken.

## Operating Context

The product is intended for Thai small-business owners working across marketplace and social channels. The demo uses a family snack shop with limited staff and a high volume of customer messages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current project is a browser UI mockup with route-based pages and shared shell/components.
- The demo can present sample sales, review, and customer-feedback data and simulated AI interactions.
- It does not connect to marketplaces, accounting systems, messaging, or a live AI service.
- Demo content and generated histories must be identified as sample data; do not present synthetic values as live shop records.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: BrandCare AI.
- The interface is in Thai.
- The demo shop is “ขนมบ้านสวน”; its voice is warm and friendly, referring to itself as “บ้านสวน” and to customers as “คุณลูกค้า”.

## Evidence on Hand

- The demo shop, products, sales figures, channel shares, customer comments, and issue narrative are maintained in the project's mockup plan and shared demo data.
- All shop, customer, and marketplace records in this project are fictional examples.

## Product Principles

- Connect customer feedback to business outcomes.
- Help the owner identify the next useful action quickly.
- Make evidence and sample-data status clear.
- Keep the owner in control of consequential actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Use semantic controls with accessible names and visible keyboard focus.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences used by the shared interface.
